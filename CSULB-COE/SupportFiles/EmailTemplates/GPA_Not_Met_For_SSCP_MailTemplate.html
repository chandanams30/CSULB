To enter the Single Subject Credential Program (SSCP) an overall G.P.A. of 2.67, or 2.75 for the last 60 semester units, is required. Your transcripts have been reviewed and your G.P.A. does not meet these requirements.

We will consider your application for admission once you have met this requirement and encourage you to consider the following options as pathways to entering our program. Depending on your current G.P.A., the grades you receive this semester may be enough to raise your G.P.A. However, if this is not the case you will need to either petition to be admitted to the program with a low GPA or take additional upper division courses here at CSULB to raise your GPA. Please feel free to contact the Teacher Preparation Advising Center if you need assistance in calculating your current or projecting your future G.P.A.

To assist with completing your Single Subject Credential and improving your G.P.A., you may enroll in SSCP Co-Requisite Courses and/or courses you may need to meet subject matter competence. Co-requisite courses (EDSP 355B; EDP 301/302/305; HSC 411B and Level I Technology) are required program courses and are open to students who have yet to be admitted to the credential program. Please note: Only students who are admitted to the credential program are eligible to enroll in SSCP Core Courses (EDSE 435/436/457 & EDSS 450).

Please speak with your EDSS 300 instructor and subject area advisor about your situation and how to re-apply once you have met the G.P.A. requirement. Credential Program Advisors are also available in the Teacher Preparation Advising Center to answer questions and assist you with this process. Please call 562.985.1105 to speak with one of us or make an advising appointment.
