From: Office of Clinical Practice

The California State University, Long Beach, College of Education thanks you for welcoming our pre-service teacher candidate to your classroom. This letter verifies the candidate has successfully submitted a valid CTC Certificate of Clearance and TB Test which are on file in our office. You may request a copy of these items and the student’s CSULB Campus ID for your records.

This candidate is enrolled in initial program coursework designed to provide experience and reflective opportunities as related to the normal workings of classrooms and school activities. The candidate will be completing several fieldwork reflections and submitting a log of their hours based on individual course expectations as outlined in their course syllabus. Appropriate fieldwork experiences may include:

  • Observations on classroom management, classroom activities, lesson development, assessment, instructional differentiation, and student support
  • Assisting the teacher with class activities, grading, tutoring as well as organizing and preparing class materials
  • Participating in professional development activities and attending extra-curricular activities

It is the responsibility of the candidate to share the expectations outlined in their assigned syllabus related to these hours as well as observe the total number of hours specified. You are free to arrange the observation schedule at your convenience.

CSULB is committed to the training and development of future teachers under the requirements of California Senate Bill 2042, which incorporates key Teaching Performance Expectations. As part of these requirements, each candidate must acquire an understanding of how to plan instruction and, in particular, how to connect instructional planning to student characteristics for academic learning. This aspect of the candidate’s experience in your classroom should expose them to learning how to identify focus students, such as those with particular academic challenges, and how to adapt lessons for those focus students.

We appreciate your support and guidance of this prospective teacher and value your feedback. Your cooperation in verifying the candidate’s hours is greatly appreciated. After each visit/observation, you will receive an email from no-reply@calstates4.com that contains a link to view and approve the candidate’s hours. If you have any questions or need any assistance in approving the hours, please email us at ced-s4@csulb.edu.

I wish you a successful semester and thank you again for all of your support.
