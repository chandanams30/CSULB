Single Subject Credential Program
Student Teaching Midterm Evaluation Tool for Cooperating Teachers

This form is aligned with the California “Teaching Performance Expectations” (TPEs) #1-7 from the Standards of Quality and Effectiveness for Teacher Preparation Programs. Candidates should be assessed as beginning practice student teachers, rather than being compared to experienced educators.


Use the Following Rating Rubric:
NC= Not Consistent with Standards Expectations for Beginning Practice Little or no evidence of effective teaching practice
D= Developing Beginning Practice Some evidence of effective teaching practice
P= Proficient Beginning Practice Substantial evidence of effective teaching practice
E= Exceptional Beginning Practice Consistent, extensive, high-quality evidence of effective teaching practice
N/O= Not Observed Evidence not observed or available currently, not a negative rating

Note 1: N/O may appear more frequently on the Mid-Term Evaluation. It should appear only rarely on the Final Evaluation.

Note 2: The Mid-Term Evaluation is for formative assessment only. A rating of “Not Consistent with Standard Expectations (NC)” for an indicator in any area denotes a critical need for improvement and may require a Student Success Action Plan to continue student teaching.
